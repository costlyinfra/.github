## 🔥 Who We Are

CostlyInfra.com is where **cloud infrastructure, AI, and FinOps meet**.
We focus on one thing: **making AI and cloud systems economically efficient**.

We turn complex infrastructure costs into **clear, actionable insights** — so teams can scale without burning money.

We are also building a growing community of engineers, builders, and operators at [r/costlyinfra](https://www.reddit.com/r/costlyinfra), where we discuss real-world challenges, share optimization techniques, and learn from each other.

## The Costly Truth of Cloud & AI

* Runaway GPU and LLM inference costs
* Poor utilization and over-provisioned compute
* Complex billing with hidden costs
* Lack of visibility into real unit economics
